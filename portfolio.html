About

My name is Israel. I'm from Brazil and I hate video games. I eat nails for breakfast and tame Siberian tigers in my spare free time. I like video games and I know how to kill skeletons. This is the "personal" version of my portfolio, meant to both cover the "Coleção de criações" and give a closer look into my personality, humor, hobbies, and whatnots. I've always hated when I have to fill out an "About me" section in job applications and such with generic, AI sounding, bland text because that is what sounds good in a curriculum and that’s what an AI would mark as "professional". So in here I wanted to add a little piece of myself, my blood, sweat, tears and many other bodily juices, in the hopes that whoever reviews it sees me as an actual individual.
